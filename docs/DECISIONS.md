- **2026-11-10** · decision · ALSA PCMs are opened only through alsa-lib with SND_PCM_NONBLOCK, only when no sound server runs (PipeWire, PulseAudio or JACK socket or process, checked without connecting) and only when /proc shows a free sub-device; ranges are read with hw_params_any and the handle is closed; parameters are never installed. - **2026-11-10** · decision · Control devices (/dev/snd/controlC*) may be opened to list and read jack, IEC958 and ELD elements; nothing is written, locked or subscribed; check-passive.sh allows them and fails on any PCM opened while a server runs or any blocking PCM open — _why:_ jack and IEC958 state are only exposed as controls; reading is non-exclusive _(by claude)_
